**What it shows:**
- **The manager's proposed words are too wide.** For finding 1 they read "a declared function, a `useCallback` result or an object property holding a function, given by reference". Those words still cover F1c (an accessor), G3 (a class field) and G2 (a literal given later), and all three pass.
- **Why, in the code:**
  - a method or accessor declaration is neither an expression nor an inline function, so neither comparison sees it (`:451`, `:462`, `:469`);
  - the by-reference rule sees a function only where the function is itself the expression passed or assigned: `line`, `view.line`, or a `key: value` property of a literal that has a contextual type.
- **So the words must name syntax, not categories.**
